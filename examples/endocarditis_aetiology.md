## Endocarditis – Classification and Aetiology

### Classification

* **By course:** acute (S. aureus, days) vs subacute (viridans streptococci, weeks–months)
* **By valve:** native valve, prosthetic valve (early < 1 year, late > 1 year), device-related
* **Non-infective:** Libman–Sacks (SLE), marantic (malignancy)

### Aetiology

| Organism | Typical setting |
| --- | --- |
| *Staphylococcus aureus* | IV drug use, prosthetic valves, healthcare-associated |
| Viridans streptococci | Dental procedures, native valves |
| *Enterococcus* | GU / GI procedures, elderly |
| HACEK group | Culture-negative IE |

### Risk factors

* Previous IE, prosthetic valve, congenital heart disease
* Rheumatic heart disease, degenerative valve disease
