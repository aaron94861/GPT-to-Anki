# Aortic Stenosis

## Definition
Narrowing of the aortic valve orifice causing obstruction to left ventricular outflow.

## Aetiology and classification
- Calcific degeneration (most common, > 65 years)
- Bicuspid aortic valve (younger patients)
- Rheumatic heart disease

## Pathogenesis
Pressure overload leads to concentric LV hypertrophy, then diastolic dysfunction and eventually failure.

## Physical examination and signs and symptoms
- Triad: **angina, syncope, dyspnoea**
- Slow-rising pulse, narrow pulse pressure
- Ejection systolic murmur radiating to the carotids

## Diagnosis
1. Echocardiography (valve area < 1 cm² = severe)
2. ECG: LVH
3. Cardiac catheterisation if unclear

## Management and treatment
- Asymptomatic: watchful waiting with regular echo
- Symptomatic severe AS: **surgical AVR or TAVI**
